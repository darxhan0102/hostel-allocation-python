Disappointment Explanations ("The Hard Part")

Under the defensible rules, one-way requests are treated as non-mutual ("a crush, not an agreement"), and roommate groups exceeding 4 beds are split with the lowest-ranked candidate bumped.

Run allocation to see rationale.

Fairness & Viable Occupancy Rules

  • 1. Mutual Agreements Only: Only confirmed reciprocal roommate requests are grouped together.
  • 2. Strict Total Ordering: (-merit, -year, id) eliminates all ties, guaranteeing byte-identical reproducibility.
  • 3. No Lonely Quads: Solitary occupants in 3-bed or 4-bed rooms are repaired by either moving them to a smaller room or pulling in a donor from a low-occupancy room.
